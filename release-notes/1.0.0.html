Wanderfund 1.0.0

First release

Added

  • Trip planner with a friendly five-step wizard: city search with autocomplete, multi-city stops, fixed or flexible dates, travellers, travel style and accommodation.
  • Budget engine with a bundled dataset of 181 destinations (estimates at three travel styles), seasonal pricing, distance-based flight estimates, insurance, eSIM, shopping and an emergency buffer, all converted to your home currency.
  • Smart suggestions (hostel nights, shoulder season, food budget) and a Tight / Realistic / Comfortable reality check on every budget.
  • Date Finder: a 12-month value calendar and the top 5 date windows from your home airport, using seasons, NSW school holidays, public holidays, weather history, day of week and booking timing. Optional live flight prices with your own SerpApi or Duffel key, plus daily price watches.
  • Exchange: live ECB rates via Frankfurter, a converter, 90-day charts compared with the average, rate alerts and a travel money planner.
  • Study Exchange mode with a monthly budget, income and funding (including OS-HELP), shortfall or surplus, and a semester timeline.
  • Savings goals with progress rings, weekly/fortnightly/monthly targets, projections, a "what if" slider, reminders and milestone confetti.
  • Expenses in any currency with the day's rate stored, receipt scanning, cost splitting and settle up, a daily budget tracker and bank CSV import.
  • Reports with interactive charts, PDF trip reports and CSV export.
  • Day-by-day itinerary with maps, and packing, documents and before-you-go checklists (with passport expiry warnings).
  • Optional "Ask Wanderfund" assistant using your own Claude API key.
  • Dashboard with countdown, savings, payments due, rates, alerts and a world map; a menu bar helper; onboarding; JSON backups; and automatic updates.
